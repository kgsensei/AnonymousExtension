Blacklist Update & Fingerprinter Crackdown

11/11/26 DMY

The AnonymousExtension blacklist has had a major update once again! It's been getting updates close to once a week now, believe it or not. Today marks the beginning of a new crackdown on fingerprinters, largly thanks to the work done at fingerprint.garden. If you haven't seen it you should definitely check it out!

With that being said, there were roughly 60 new fingerprint-specific rules added. Things shouldn't break, but sometimes websites are really insistant on fingerprinting you, in that case just disable AnonymousExtension for that specific website in the control panel.

Speaking of the control panel, a new top secret feature has been added. Clicking the AnonymousExtension logo three times will force a blacklist update. AnonymousExtension automatically updates once a day but if you want new updates faster this is a great way to keep up!
It can also be used for debugging purposes, but that's completely different.
